**While Hyperliquid, the decentralized perpetual futures exchange, continues to gain popularity among traders, a recent analysis has revealed a significant imbalance in trader profitability, with only 14% of traders achieving positive returns.**

While a small group of traders have made substantial profits (over eight figures), the majority of Hyperliquid traders are not seeing significant gains.

## How Many Hyperliquid Traders Are Actually Making Money?

According to the Hyperdash data, just 135 out of a sample of [1,000 traders have recorded](https://beincrypto.com/james-wynn-trades-spark-controversy/) profitable outcomes. The average daily profit or loss (PnL) across all traders is a loss of $5,600.

This suggests that most [Hyperliquid users are incurring losses](https://beincrypto.com/andrew-tate-james-wynn-leverage-trap-hyperliquid/), a trend consistent with the high-risk nature of leveraged trading.

![Hyperliquid Trader Profitability](https://assets.beincrypto.com/img/9mGUQLKe7zJLWa9Z57Q5aTPgMmY=/smart/43ef2984b6ec4ac7bb4d24fb5ea0a168)


Hyperliquid Trader Profitability. Source: [Hyperdash](https://hyperdash.info/top-traders)

Furthermore, crypto analyst DeFi Mochi highlighted on X (formerly Twitter) that only 170 traders on Hyperliquid have accumulated profits exceeding $10 million. Meanwhile, 1,589 traders have earned over $1 million. These figures include both profits from perpetual trading and rewards from airdrops.

However, Mochi pointed out that many of [these 8-figure profit earners have a return](https://beincrypto.com/learn/how-to-trade-hyperliquid/) on investment (ROI) of less than 200%. This relatively low ROI suggests that these top traders likely started with significant initial capital, using their existing resources to leverage larger positions and amplify their profits.

> “That’s just people that got a good AirDrop and sit on it lol,” a user [commented](https://x.com/Zdeadex/status/1934295717075415446).

This raises questions about the sustainability of profit concentration among a small cohort of [well-capitalized traders on the platform](https://beincrypto.com/hyperliquid-surge-daily-volume-james-wynn/).

Despite the profitability challenges for most traders, Hyperliquid continues to dominate the decentralized finance (DeFi) sector. [BeInCrypto reported recently that the platform’s](https://beincrypto.com/hybrid-protocols-hyperliquid-beat-full-decentralization/) hybrid decentralization model has attracted substantial investor capital and boosted trust.

[Data from Dune Analytics further highlighted](https://beincrypto.com/learn/dune-analytics) Hyperliquid’s strong position. The platform commands over 60% of the market share among perps platforms.

![Hyperliquid Perps Market Share](https://assets.beincrypto.com/img/S0Z2Oo3lyt_RfTUYKl9fE5RlH9k=/smart/55b1e482489e4666ab7ea4aa2f9a5cd9)


Hyperliquid Perps Market Share. Source: [Dune](https://dune.com/uwusanauwu/perps)

In addition, Hyperliquid boasts 499,231 users. Over the past 30 days, the platform has processed $188 billion in trading volume, generating $37.61 million in fees, highlighting strong usage in the DeFi space.

Thus, while not all traders may experience profits on the platform, Hyperliquid remains a preferred choice for many, as evidenced by its market position, substantial user base, and [continued growth in the decentralized finance sector](https://beincrypto.com/coinbase-clo-paul-grewal-sec-reversal-defi-custody/).
